- `dst_host_serror_rate` and `dst_host_srv_serror_rate` were both 1.0, showing the same failure pattern concentrated on a single destination host and service.
- `logged_in` was 0, confirming no session was ever established.
- `count` was 300, indicating a very high volume of connections in the observation window.

1. Validate whether `192.168.1.47` is an approved scanner, load tester, or internal monitoring host.
2. Review firewall and IDS logs for the volume of half-open (SYN-only) connections from the source.
3. Enable SYN-flood protection / rate-limiting on the targeted destination host, or temporarily block the source if the activity is unauthorized.
4. Preserve flow records and packet captures for post-incident review.
